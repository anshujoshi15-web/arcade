How It Works

Enter with a $1 collectible ticket or claim one free entry each week. Five different wallets win in a qualifying round, with up to $1,500 for first place.

Enter the weekly round

  1. Connect one wallet and reuse it each week to build your collection and streak. Entry requires an eligible wallet and compliance with the round’s age and location rules.
  2. Buy tickets for 1 USDC each or claim one free ticket per wallet per week. You can buy up to 25 paid tickets at a time and receive up to 100 entries per round, including free and bonus tickets.
  3. Nominate a charity when you enter. Your first nomination counts once. The listed charity with the most nominations receives the round’s charity share. Ties follow the published charity list order.

The planned network is Base. Each NFT uses the ERC-721 standard. The local practice edition uses test funds.

Tickets and collectibles

Each round can issue up to 10,000 ticket NFTs, with serials from 00001 to 10000. Paid, free, and bonus entries share that limit. Each ticket enters one round and stays in your wallet as a collectible afterward.

The first 100 serials are Gold. The first 1,000 paid tickets are marked Early. These collectible traits carry the same draw weight as other tickets. Tickets have no promised resale value.

  • 2 consecutive weeks earn a streak mark on your ticket.
  • 4 consecutive weeks unlock one free bonus ticket in the following round.
  • 8 consecutive weeks earn a Streak Keeper collectible.
  • 12 consecutive weeks earn a Gold Streak collectible and first-hour access to available Gold serials in the following round.

A missed week resets the streak. Streak badge NFTs are separate collectibles.

How prizes are calculated

Each dollar in paid ticket sales sends 34¢ to charity, 33¢ to House operations, and 33¢ to the prize pool. The five prizes scale with actual paid sales.

Free and bonus tickets add entries without adding to the prize pool. If all 10,000 serials are issued and 500 are free or bonus tickets, the 9,500 paid entries fund $3,135 in prizes, including $1,425 for first place.

The draw requires 6,000 paid tickets. That takes at least 60 buying wallets at 100 paid tickets each, or 600 at 10 each. If the Sunday checkpoint arrives below 6,000 paid tickets, the draw is postponed. Sales stay open, tickets stay valid, and the full pot carries forward. Purchases are final.

Watch the draw and claim

The weekly schedule opens Monday at 10 a.m., closes Sunday at 6 p.m., and draws Sunday at 8 p.m., all in Mountain Time. A sellout closes entries early and can bring the draw forward.

The planned draw uses publicly verifiable randomness. In a full 10,000-entry round, one ticket has a 1 in 10,000 chance at first place. Each wallet can win once. The chance of winning any prize also depends on how tickets are distributed across wallets.

Check the results by serial. The wallet that held the winning NFT at the draw snapshot has 30 days to claim. Unclaimed prizes go to the round’s selected charity.
