    Round rules and funding

    10,000 serials per round. Up to 25 paid entries per purchase and 100 entries per wallet. One weekly free entry has the same draw weight as a paid ticket. Five different wallets win. The draw requires 6,000 paid tickets.

    Paid entries fund charity (34%), operations (33%) and prizes (33%). Free tickets count toward capacity. Prize amounts scale with paid entries.
