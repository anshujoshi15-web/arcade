General Rules

These rules apply across House Arcade. When a game has a more specific rule, that game’s rule applies. Read the individual rules before playing.

Why we play

The House of Joshi is a business. It collects game fees and makes donations from those fees. Players can nominate recipients under each game’s rules. Kindred (KIND) is associated with the House’s give-back efforts.

How paid entries are split

The Arcade’s general split is 34% for the House’s donation, 33% for House operations, and 33% for player prizes. Each game publishes its own rules, including its split and any specific terms, before opening.

Payments, prizes, and bonuses

Game fees are paid in crypto. Main prizes are shown in USD value and paid in Base ETH. Free entries add no revenue and do not count toward paid-entry gates.

Where a game offers a bonus, the winner can choose EVO, Kaboom, King Joshi, Queen Joshi, or KIND. House tokens carry no promised value or buy-back. A future switch to KIND-only bonuses would be announced before the affected week.

Games and their rules

  • House Raffle uses a ticket draw. If the round misses its paid-ticket threshold, the draw is postponed and the pot carries forward. Postponement leaves purchases in the round. The round can postpone up to four weeks. Refunds apply only if the House cancels or formally closes it without a draw. The House then refunds original buyers in full in USDC.
  • Puzzle Print asks players to collect pieces and complete a print.
  • Spin Wheel offers instant wins and jackpot entries. Instant wins from free spins pay in House tokens.
  • Weekly Bingo awards the player pool to the first full card, subject to the game’s rules.
  • Treasure Hunt asks players to collect clues and find the treasure.

Who can play

  • You must be 18 or older to play, including through a free entry route.
  • Every game offers free entry. No purchase is necessary to take part or win.
  • Follow the entry caps in each game’s rules. Caps apply to both paid and free entry.
  • Price, caps, split, prizes, and the way winners are chosen are published before opening and stay fixed while the game or round is running.

Fair play and conduct

  • Do not use bots, scripts, multiple accounts to bypass caps, or any other method to manipulate an outcome.
  • Do not coordinate with other players to fix a result.
  • Treat other players and the House with courtesy. Rude, discourteous, and unsportsmanlike conduct is prohibited.
  • If something appears wrong, contact Arcade support and give the House an opportunity to review it.

The House of Joshi may end gameplay without prior warning when it suspects cheating or prohibited conduct. This may apply to a player, a game, or the whole Arcade. The House may remove entries or winnings gained by cheating.

Soft opening and paid entries

House Arcade is open for testing and community building. Before a game sells any entries, an attorney must review that game’s rules and the required licences must be in place. Until those steps are complete, the game’s documents are drafts for review. If applicable law conflicts with these rules, the law governs and the House will publish corrected rules before opening.
