Planned game rules and prizes

The proposed pool contains 25,000 pieces, with 1,000 copies of each design. The proposed price is $2 a piece. Players can draw up to 25 per transaction and hold up to 50 at a time. One free piece per wallet per week uses the same pool.

The prize race pays by finishing order after claims open and the next prize is funded. The ladder is $5,000, $4,000, $3,000, six prizes of $664.29, and $664.26 for tenth place. A winning claim burns a full set and issues the completed artwork. The first 6,000 paid pieces fund $12,000 in prize escrow.

Practice trades simulate a peer accepting a swap. Practice draws and claims use browser storage and have no cash value.
