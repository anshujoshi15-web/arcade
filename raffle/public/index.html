    Raffle rules, eligibility and odds

    Each round offers 10,000 serials. A purchase can contain 1–25 tickets, and each wallet can receive up to 100 entries. Free and bonus tickets use the same serial pool, with up to 4,000 combined so 6,000 slots remain available for paid entry. Five distinct wallets win. A single ticket in a full round has a 1 in 10,000 chance at first place.

    If the round has fewer than 6,000 paid tickets at its first Sunday sales close, sales stay open and tickets and prize funds carry forward for up to four weeks after that first Sunday close. If the round is still below the threshold at the four-week limit, entries close and the House must formally end the round. The House can cancel at any time before it requests the draw. If the House cancels or formally ends the round without a draw, original buyers receive full USDC refunds and free entries are void. Postponement alone does not trigger refunds. Gold, Early and streak marks are collectible traits and carry the same draw weight as other serials.

    Each entrant’s first nomination counts once. Tied charities follow their published order in the nomination menu. The charity receives 34% of paid entries, the House receives 33%, and the five prizes total 33%. Winners have 30 days to claim. Unclaimed prizes go to the winning charity.

    Entry is for adults meeting the round’s age and location requirements. Check your wallet’s entry status. A ticket has no promised resale value.
