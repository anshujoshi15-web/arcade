Privacy & wallet data

Wallet connections

With your permission, the site reads your public wallet address from your wallet app. A connection lets a game identify your account. The public wallet picker uses Reown AppKit and WalletConnect. Your wallet handles transaction approvals.

Public blockchain activity

Raffle transactions, NFT ownership, and claims are public on the configured blockchain. The app uses the configured RPC provider to read round and wallet information.

Practice data and admin sessions

Puzzle Print stores its practice collection and nominations in your browser on this device. Clearing site data removes that progress. The restricted admin dashboard uses a short-lived session cookie after the House wallet signs in.
