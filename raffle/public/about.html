About House Arcade

The House of Joshi

House Arcade is the game hub from The House of Joshi, a business that operates games and makes donations from game fees.

House Raffle issues numbered NFT tickets on Base. The other seven games are available as free practice from the games list. House Raffle is the only game accepting paid entries.

See the General Rules for eligibility and game policies.
