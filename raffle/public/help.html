Help

Connect on a phone or tablet

Open House Arcade in the browser inside your Ethereum wallet app, then tap Connect wallet. On desktop, use a browser with an Ethereum wallet extension. House Raffle shows the network for the current round.

Enter House Raffle

Check the round status and wallet eligibility on the raffle page. Read How It Works for entry limits, postponements, prize claims, and the charity split.

Play Puzzle Print

The practice game saves your collection on the current device. You can play without connecting a wallet. Clearing site data removes your practice progress.

Report an issue

Use the House Arcade issue tracker for technical problems. Share the game, device, and error message. Keep your recovery phrase and private keys private.
