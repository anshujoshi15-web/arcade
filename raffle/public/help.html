Help

Connect on a phone or tablet

Tap Connect wallet and choose your wallet. On a phone or tablet, the connection can open your wallet app. On desktop, use a wallet extension or scan the WalletConnect QR code from your phone. House Raffle shows the network for the current round.

Enter House Raffle

Check the round status and wallet eligibility on the raffle page. Read How It Works for entry limits, postponements, prize claims, and the charity split.

Upcoming games

Puzzle Print, Spin Wheel, Weekly Bingo, and Treasure Hunt are coming soon. See the games list for availability.

Report an issue

Use the House Arcade issue tracker for technical problems. Share the game, device, and error message. Keep your recovery phrase and private keys private.
