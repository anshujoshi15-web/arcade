Help

Read the Arcade General Rules before playing.

Connect on a phone or tablet

Tap Connect wallet and choose your wallet. On a phone or tablet, the connection can open your wallet app. On desktop, use a wallet extension or scan the WalletConnect QR code from your phone. House Raffle shows the network for the current round.

Enter House Raffle

Connect your wallet and open Wallet entry status to check paid and free entry. Read How It Works for entry limits, postponements, prize claims, and the charity split.

Upcoming games

Puzzle Print, Spin Wheel, Weekly Bingo, and Treasure Hunt are coming soon. See the games list for availability.

Report an issue

Tell the House which game or page is affected. Include your device, browser, and any error message. Keep recovery phrases and private keys private.

You can also email Arcade support.
